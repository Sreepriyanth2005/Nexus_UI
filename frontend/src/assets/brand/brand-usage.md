# Nexus Academy Brand Usage

Logo, favicon and design system rules for the Nexus Academy portal. Every color, size and surface rule below is grounded in the kit tokens.

## The Mark

The Nexus mark is a modern geometric hexagon with dynamic nodal connections and the stylized central "N" monogram.
The favicon, `favicon.svg`, is an optimized vector rendition with honey gold nodes on an ink ground.

Approved colors:

| Finish | Hex | Token |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#141414` | `--ink` |
| White | `#ffffff` | `--paper` |
| Honey gold | `#c99b2e` | `--c-honey` |

## Logo Variants

| Variant | File | Use on |
|---|---|---|
| Ink on light (default) | `nexus-logo-ink.svg` | White or light paper surfaces (`--paper` `#ffffff`, `--paper-2` `#f6f6f4`). Topbar, cards, proof and print pages. |
| White on dark | `nexus-logo-white.svg` | Dark backgrounds and photo heroes (`--ink` `#141414`, or over `auth-bg.png`). The auth pages use this. |
